# Supplementary Figure legends (post-audit figures)

**Supplementary Figure 1 | LGSN and CRYGS lesions are reproduced by an independent annotation method and in independently sequenced assemblies.**
Each panel shows the human protein (**a**, LGSN, 509 codons; **b**, CRYGS, 178 codons). For each subterranean species, the first track gives the inactivating mutations recorded by TOGA (open symbols). The tracks below it give the lesions found by aligning the human protein directly to the genome with miniprot (filled symbols), in the assembly TOGA used and, where available, in independently sequenced assemblies (†). Triangles mark premature stop codons; diamonds mark frameshifts. Positions are human codon coordinates. A miniprot lesion counts as matching TOGA if it lies within ±2 codons of a TOGA lesion of the same kind. "Not aligned at default settings" means miniprot returned no alignment within ±2 Mb of the syntenic locus. These loci are not confirmed here and are not counted as evidence either way. The naked mole-rat LGSN stop codon at codon 121 occurs in all three assemblies (2012, 2025 and 2026). Source data: results/c5/lesions.csv, toga_lesions.csv and locus_validation.csv; code: scripts/c5_locus_validation.py and scripts/make_figures.py (`supp_lesion_map`).

**Supplementary Figure 2 | Genome-wide single-origin support for subterranean candidates under three loss codings.**
Columns show the three loss codings: uncertain losses counted as lost (primary analysis); confident losses only, with uncertain calls treated as missing; and uncertain losses counted as intact. The number of genes loss-tested under each coding is given in the column header. Colour shows −log10 of the conservative Benjamini–Hochberg-adjusted P′ from the intersection–union (every-origin) test. P′ is the Monte Carlo intersection–union P for genes called by the convergence screen and 1 for all other genes, with BH applied across all genes tested under that coding. Outlined cells pass q ≤ 0.1. Dots mark genes that the screen did not call under that coding; dashes mark genes that were not loss-tested. Asterisks mark ocular genes. Rows show every gene called under at least one coding, ordered by adjusted P across the columns. LGSN and CRYGS pass in all three codings. Six further genes (LCTL, RSKR, BFSP1, CRYBA1, ABCA4, DDIT4L) pass in both alternative codings, which have reduced multiple-testing burdens. Source data: results/audit2/iut_{primary,Lonly,ULintact}_subterranean; code: scripts/make_figures.py (`supp_coding_robustness`).
